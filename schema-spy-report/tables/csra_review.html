csra_review

-1 rows


Description

One cell sharing risk assessment or review for a prisoner. Holds only what is common to both the new DPS journeys and reviews migrated from NOMIS; legacy-only NOMIS detail hangs off csra_review_nomis and the new-model captured answers off csra_assessment_stage. A prisoner accumulates many of these over time - the one that counts today is the projection in csra_current_rating.
